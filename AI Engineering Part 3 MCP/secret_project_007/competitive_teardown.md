# Project Falcon Competitive Teardown

## Project Falcon Summary

Project Falcon is a tech news and discussion community featuring AI avatars who provide commentary. It combines the spirit of Hacker News with the entertainment dynamics of a Late Night Talk Show, but in a written format with AI commentators. The project bets on engaging AI avatars to become fan favorites through their unique dynamics. The launch target is the end of Q3 with a fixed timeline and no scope creep allowed. "Falcon" is a placeholder name; speed to market is a critical moat against rumored competitors building similar products.
